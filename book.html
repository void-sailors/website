The Book

VoidSailors

Where Truth Lies

Dexter Blackwell has a loaned dorm room, a controller worn smooth, and nothing to fall back on if he loses.

Get It on Amazon Read a Free Chapter

The Story

Inside the game he becomes TenScore, a sailor under colors he never chose, in a five-day contest where nothing respawns and every crew that takes him aboard expects something back. Alliances come cheap. Trust is the one currency that holds its value, and before the Void lets him go, he will learn exactly what his costs.

Genre and Style

Fantasy GameLit where nobody levels up out of trouble. Stats matter less than decisions, and each one costs someone.

How the Game Plays

Nobody starts with a class or a race, and everybody starts broke. What a sailor carries, who crews with them, and what they are willing to give up decide who reaches Day Five.

The World

The story unfolds across hovering islands separated by the Great Deep, linked by trade routes, contested gates, and drifting hover-sail ships. Every crossing costs supplies, and the wrong crew can turn it into a one-way trip.
