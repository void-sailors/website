The Book

VoidSailors

Where Truth Lies

A fantasy GameLit novel set during a five-day competitive event, where players navigate faction politics, scarce resources, and choices that cannot be undone.

Get It on Amazon Read a Free Chapter

Overview

Progress is not measured by numbers alone. In VoidSailors it comes from trust, attention, and the consequences of every alliance a player makes or breaks.

Genre and Style

The novel blends fantasy and GameLit with progression driven by consequence rather than stat accumulation. It focuses on systems, ideology, and moral pressure, offering a grounded alternative to power-fantasy growth while keeping tension, momentum, and discovery.

Systems and Mechanics

Finite inventory, faction economics, shared resources, and attention-driven progression shape what happens to each character. Preparation, loyalty, and restraint matter as much as strength.

The World

The story unfolds across hovering islands separated by the Great Deep, linked by trade routes, contested gates, and drifting hover-sail ships. Logistics and crew composition define power, which makes every crossing a strategic decision rather than a convenience.
