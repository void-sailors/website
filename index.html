The Four Factions

Power in VoidSailors comes from factions, not morality bars. Traders, fleets, pirates and drifters each run on their own economics. Alliances are temporary, incentives are visible, and every faction decision changes who gets resources, protection, and a chance to survive.

Crew and Rivals

The Heroes

Picking the right ally can matter more than picking the right weapon. Here, heroes are defined by trust, shared risk, and loyalty in a world that never resets. Friends open paths. The wrong ones close them forever.

What You Carry

Sailor’s Inventory

Inventory in VoidSailors is finite, visible, and meaningful. Cargo decides what you can do, who you can help, and what you must abandon.

Every item stands for labor, trade, or sacrifice, which turns packing the hold into a strategic and moral choice rather than a background mechanic.

Each item hides a trial. Win one and the next unlocks. Start with the cannonballs.
