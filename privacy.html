The Fine Print

Privacy Policy

VoidSailors keeps very little about you, and nothing it keeps is for sale.

Effective October 16, 2026

The Short Version

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies.
  • Your quiz result, faction colors, trial progress and reading settings stay on your own device.
  • An email address reaches us only when you join Mailing in a Bottle or claim a book through BookFunnel.
  • We never sell, rent or trade personal data.
  • Unsubscribe in one click, or ask us to erase everything at any time.

Who We Are

This website, voidsailors.com, belongs to author E. J. Perry, who is responsible for any personal data it handles. Throughout this policy, “we” and “us” mean E. J. Perry, and “you” means anyone visiting the site, joining the newsletter or downloading a book.

What We Collect

Your email address, but only when you hand it over: by signing up for Mailing in a Bottle, our newsletter, or by entering it on a BookFunnel page to claim a free book or an advance reader copy (ARC). We ask for nothing else, such as your name, phone or postal address.

Basic server records. Like every website, ours is delivered by hosting companies whose servers note each request: your IP address, browser type, the page asked for and the time. They use these records to run and secure their networks. We do not combine them with anything else or use them to identify visitors.

Nothing more. The site runs no analytics, advertising pixels, social-media trackers or tracking cookies. Fonts, images and the trailer come from voidsailors.com itself, so visiting a page sends no request to Google, Facebook or any other third party.

What Stays on Your Device

A few features remember your choices using your browser’s local storage. This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it:

  • the faction the Gate assigned you and the sailor name you typed, so your crest card can be rebuilt;
  • the faction colors you picked or earned for the site;
  • your progress through the inventory trials and the scroll riddle;
  • reader settings for the free chapter: text size, Paper mode and the page where you stopped.
  • if you add the VoidSailors card to your home screen, a copy of that one page and its pictures, so it opens without a signal.

Clearing your browser’s site data erases all of it. The site works without it; it simply forgets your progress. Sharing a crest card happens through your phone’s own share sheet, and the picture is created in your browser rather than uploaded to us.

Mailing in a Bottle

When you sign up, your address goes straight to Sender.net, the service that stores the list and delivers our emails, along with the address of the page you signed up on. Sender then emails you a link to confirm, and you join the list only after you click it. No Sender script loads on our pages; your browser contacts Sender only at the moment you press the signup button.

We use your address solely to send launch news, cover reveals and updates about VoidSailors and future books by E. J. Perry. Sender may record whether an email was opened or a link was clicked, which helps us tell if messages are arriving. Every email carries an unsubscribe link, and one click removes you.

Our legal basis for this is your consent, which you can withdraw whenever you like.

Free Books and ARCs via BookFunnel

Reader magnets, free stories and advance reader copies are delivered by BookFunnel. When you claim one, BookFunnel collects your email address and details about your reading device so it can send the right file and help if a download fails. BookFunnel handles that information under its own privacy policy.

  • Newsletter sign-ups. If a BookFunnel page states that claiming the book also joins Mailing in a Bottle, your address is passed to our list and treated exactly as described above. You may unsubscribe at once and keep the book.
  • ARC readers. When you request an advance copy, we may see your email address and any review links you choose to share with us. We use them only to deliver the copy and follow up about your review, never for anything else.

Who Else Sees It

We share personal data only with the services that make the site work, each limited to its own task:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Cloudflare manages our domain.
  • Sender.net stores the mailing list and sends the newsletter.
  • BookFunnel delivers free books and ARCs.

Some of these companies process data in the United States or the European Union. Beyond them, we disclose information only if the law requires it. We never sell personal data or share it for advertising.

Links Elsewhere

Buttons for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Amazon and similar sites take you away from voidsailors.com. Those platforms apply their own privacy rules once you arrive, so please review theirs.

How Long We Keep It

Your email address stays on the list until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove it. Hosting companies keep their server records for the limited period their own policies set.

Your Rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it or stop using it. Readers in the European Union, the United Kingdom, California and other places with privacy laws have these rights by statute, and you may also complain to your local data-protection authority. Send a request using the contact details below and we will answer within 30 days.

Young Readers

VoidSailors is written for young adults, yet the newsletter is open only to people aged 13 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has signed up, tell us and we will delete the address.

Keeping It Safe

Every page loads over an encrypted HTTPS connection, no password or access key for the mailing list appears in the website’s code, and access to the subscriber list is limited to the author. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we hold to a minimum so there is little to lose.

Changes to This Policy

If our practices change, we will update this page and the date at the top. A significant change will also be announced in Mailing in a Bottle before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data can reach E. J. Perry by replying to any Mailing in a Bottle email or by sending a direct message on Instagram.
